Our electrical designs need testing before being implemented, and this testing requires specialist equipment. One of Electronic Test and Power Systems' (ETPS) speciality industries is motorsport, and they have provided Team Bath Racing electric with industry standard testing equipment which allows us to validate our battery systems in isolation to the other systems.
