Procter & Chester (Measurements) Ltd (PCM) is a third-generation family business that has specialised in load measurement since 1983. From its base in Kenilworth, Warwickshire, the company designs and manufactures market-leading load cells, load pins and torque transducers. Their specialist strain gauging service supports everything from static tests to fatigue programmes, in sectors as demanding as motorsport, aerospace, defence and nuclear. PCM also runs a force and torque calibration laboratory accredited by UKAS to ISO/IEC 17025, alongside bespoke design, hire and repair services. Whatever the project, engineers worldwide rely on PCM for precision, traceability and rapid turnaround, knowing their solutions are of superlative quality and built to stand the test of time.


As a year-on-year Platinum Sponsor, PCM gives us hands-on training in strain gauging, load measurement and calibration, together with the equipment we need to instrument our own car. Their aim is to develop us as engineers, giving us the means to validate our designs, and the simulations behind them, against real-world track data.
