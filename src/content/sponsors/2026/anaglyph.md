Anaglyph Ltd. is a UK-based composites design and manufacturing solutions specialist, celebrating 30 years on the market in 2026. The company has been serving the composites industry worldwide working closely with the automotive and aerospace sectors, supplying composites design and analysis software. Alongside its flagship product Laminate Tools, Anaglyph also offers PlyMatch, an augmented-reality technology for accurate hand layup ply placement, CoDA for preliminary analysis of composite subcomponents, and LAP (Laminate Analysis Program), based on classical laminate theory. Anaglyph software are being used in high-technology industry in more than 40 countries worldwide.
